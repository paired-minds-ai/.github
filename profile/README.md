# Paired Minds AI

**Two minds. Shared curiosity.**

Welcome to Paired Minds AI! This is our shared space for AI projects, experiments, and things we've learned along the way.

Most of our projects come from our undergraduate coursework, along with our own ideas and experiments. We use this organization to share what we've built, try new things, and keep growing our skills together.

## How We Work Together

We both contribute to our shared portfolio, but each project has a lead. The member who did most of the work on the project usually creates and manages its repository.

This doesn't mean the other member wasn't involved. We can both contribute ideas, code, experiments, and documentation, and we want our work to reflect that.

## Our Approach

We're here to learn by building, share our progress, and improve through trial and error. We also believe it's important to be honest about what worked, what didn't, and what we could do better.

## Explore Our Projects

Take a look at our repositories to see what we've been working on. More projects will be added as we learn and build together.
